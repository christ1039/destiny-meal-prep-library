Pre-portioned food guide

Build a balanced plate without guessing

Lean Proteins

Chicken breast, ground turkey, salmon, tuna, shrimp, eggs, Greek yogurt, tofu, tempeh, lean beef.

Portion: 4 to 6 oz cooked.

Carbs

Jasmine rice, brown rice, potatoes, sweet potatoes, oats, quinoa, pasta, wraps, fruit.

Portion: 1/2 to 1 cup cooked.

Fats

Avocado, olive oil, nuts, nut butter, seeds, cheese, salmon.

Portion: 1 tbsp oil or 1/4 avocado.

Vegetables/Fiber

Broccoli, green beans, spinach, zucchini, peppers, asparagus, salad greens, carrots.

Portion: 1 to 2 cups.

Flavor/Sauces

Salsa, hot sauce, Greek yogurt sauce, light teriyaki, buffalo sauce, lemon, herbs, seasoning blends.

Tip: Add flavor before adding extra calories.

Portions depend on individual goals, activity level, appetite, and health needs.
